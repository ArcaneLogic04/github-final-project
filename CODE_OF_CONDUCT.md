# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

We are committed to creating a respectful and welcoming environment for everyone.

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Communicate clearly and professionally.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, and offensive behavior.

## Enforcement

Unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. Maintainers may take appropriate action to keep the project welcoming and respectful.
